# Sample Health Insurance Policy

This sample policy is for testing the QA bot only. Replace it with real policy documents before using the application for actual policy analysis.

## Coverage

The policy covers inpatient hospitalization expenses when admission lasts at least 24 hours. Covered expenses may include room rent, nursing charges, intensive care charges, surgeon fees, diagnostic tests, medicines, and operation theatre charges.

## Waiting Periods

Pre-existing diseases are covered after a waiting period of 36 months from the policy start date.

Specified conditions such as cataract, hernia, joint replacement, and sinusitis are covered after a waiting period of 24 months.

## Exclusions

The policy does not cover cosmetic surgery, dental treatment unless caused by an accident, experimental treatment, self-inflicted injury, or treatment caused by alcohol or drug abuse.

## Claim Process

For planned hospitalization, the insured person should notify the insurer at least 72 hours before admission. For emergency hospitalization, the insurer should be notified within 24 hours of admission.

## Deductible

The policy has an annual deductible of INR 10,000. The insurer pays admissible claims only after the deductible has been met.
